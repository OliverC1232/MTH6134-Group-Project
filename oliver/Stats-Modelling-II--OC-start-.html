In the ‘Matches’ dataset, there seems to be some relation between goals_scored/opponent_rank and goals_conceded/opponent_rank. We investigate this further below by fitting a Normal Linear Model to the data with opponent_rank as the response:

model1 = lm(opponent_rank~goals_scored+goals_conceded, data=matches)
